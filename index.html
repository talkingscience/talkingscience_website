Talking Science with Anne Churchland

Talking Science is a unique opportunity to meet and talk about neuroscience with a leading researcher in an informal atmosphere - a two-day program for the MSc and PhD students of LMU, TUM, and the MPIs.

Sign up now!

About

This year's guest is Prof. Anne Churchland (Professor of Neurobiology, David Geffen School of Medicine, UCLA).

Prof. Churchland's work has reshaped how we think about the neural basis of decision-making. Her lab studies how animals combine evidence from multiple senses — sights and sounds — with internal signals such as prior expectations and biases in order to commit to a choice. Using large-scale recordings across the cortex, her group showed that neural activity during decisions is shaped not only by the task at hand but by the animal’s own uninstructed movements, a finding that changed how the field interprets trial-to-trial variability in the brain. She is a founding member of the International Brain Laboratory, a collaboration spanning dozens of labs working towards a brain-wide account of decision-making, and the creator of Anne’s List, a widely used resource for improving the representation of women in systems and computational neuroscience.

In this year’s Talking Science program, participants will be able to meet Prof. Churchland in small groups, join roundtable discussions on career choices and scientific trajectories, and attend her keynote lecture. The event provides a unique opportunity to engage with a leading figure in neuroscience.
